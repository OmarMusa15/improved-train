TEAM PROJECT · DIGITAL ELECTRONICS✳

Logic Gates

My teammates and I constructed three logic gates: NOT, AND, and OR. Logic gates process binary inputs (0 and 1) and produce outputs based on logical rules. They are essential building blocks of computers, processors, and other digital devices.We built NOT, AND, and OR gates to see how binary inputs shape digital devices.

💡 This project helped me understand how the tiny building blocks inside digital devices turn simple inputs into useful decisions.
